Eve's Space · premium nail studio, Downtown LA

Challenge: “From Marketing Activity to Profitable Appointments” · owner Eve

Context. Six years, loyal clients, real menu (Russian manicure, Gel-X, Smart Pedicure, OSEA add-ons) at 727 W 7th St. Yet chairs are 41% full and net profit fell from $5.5k (May) to $590 (Oct). Spend is spread over Google, Instagram, influencers and ClassPass. Revenue sits in Square, ClassPass and Airbnb. Nobody knows which marketing becomes a profitable appointment.

$38 vs $4margin per chair-hour, Google vs ClassPass
77 → 6influencer clients came in → came back
$7,200 → $3,866paid Instagram spend → return
One complete workflow. Goals onboarding: “$10k net a month” → Evie asks only for the integrations that goal needs → 90-day strategy with the math. “Launch ads for my best service” → the Ads agent picks the service with the most open chair-hours, writes three angles, generates visuals, scores them and keeps the strongest → one Approve → Google / Meta import files with a booking code. Gap Filler texts the best-fit clients for each empty hour.

Impact, as computed by the strategy card

  • Gap Filler +$2,369, win-back of 173 lapsed clients +$900, budget shift from influencers to Google and referrals +$1,900 a month.
  • Net profit $590 → ~$5,759 a month projected. The remaining $4,241 gap to $10k is shown, not hidden.
  • Open chair-hours this week 29 → 12. A week of content drafted in minutes, in Eve's voice.

Brow Down Studio · studio + Pro products + education

Extra challenge: “From Creative Guesswork to a Repeatable, Insight-Led Beauty Marketing Engine” · owner René

Context. Three businesses in one brand. Campaigns depend on the founder's time: she wrote the whole Fall kit launch herself in 11 hours. There is no loop from past results to the next plan, and no view of whether Brow Down shows up when customers ask Google or an AI assistant.

68% vs 2%Fall kit orders from email vs TikTok
9 → 14rank drop, “brow lamination kit”, 5.9k impressions
2–3 of 12AI-assistant answers that mention Brow Down
One complete workflow. René types one line: “Holiday gift sets, ship by Dec 15, budget $600” → Campaign Planner returns a 90-order / $8,000 plan. The channel mix comes from past-launch learnings. Assets get owners and due dates on a 3-week calendar. Copy is drafted live and checked by a brand-voice guard → approve the plan → one approval per asset. A launch retro records result vs target, and the next plan cites it.

Discovery and impact

  • 12 real customer questions are sent live to a model. Each miss becomes a task: FAQ page, llms.txt + product schema, aftercare guide.
  • Launch prep drops from hours to minutes. The team sees its assets, owners and dates.
  • Search and AI visibility become a number the team can move every Monday.

How AI is used, and why it is different

  • Agents, not dashboards. Ads, SMM and SEO agents run end to end. The owner sees the result and taps one Approve, with no micro-steps.
  • “Why?” on everything. Every number and recommendation opens a score breakdown and the math. Numbers are computed from the data. The model writes and explains.
  • Data health score 46 → 78 with a fix queue, so the owner knows what to trust.
  • Talk to Evie. Chat that tolerates typos, voice notes and a voice call. The Pixar-style owl changes look per character (calm, hype, nerd, coach) and pose per task.

Beyond one business

The same agent and codebase run a nail studio measured on chair-hours and a product + education brand measured on campaigns. Every table carries a studio id. A new business onboards in four steps: goals, integrations, strategy, first approval. The model fits salons, spas, barbers, clinics, fitness and tattoo studios. It can be priced per filled chair-hour, not per seat.

Built on all four Converge.AI products

Enter: the whole app, database, sign-in, edge functions, live AI and image generation. Framia: the animated owl and the video-ad hand-off. Covey: the 10-slide pitch deck. Combos: Evie as a chat-able AI character.
